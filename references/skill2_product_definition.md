# SkillⅡ Product Definition

## Formal Name

求职咨询备课与演练助手

Internal module name: `skill2-consultation-prep-rehearsal`

## Users

- Internal consultant
- Sales consultant
- Manager or reviewer

## Core Job

Use SkillⅠ pre-consultation diagnosis reports to help consultants prepare for a real 1v1 consultation, rehearse high-risk moments, receive evaluation, and produce an internal post-consultation follow-up plan.

## What SkillⅡ Does

- Read SkillⅠ pre-consultation report.
- Extract main direction, backup direction, high-risk direction, customer advantages, gaps, and open questions.
- Guide job screenshot collection without automated crawling.
- Convert screenshots into a job evidence matrix and direction evidence chain.
- Identify deal risks before the consultation.
- Simulate customer, parent, or payer in a full-intensity rehearsal.
- Evaluate consultant readiness after rehearsal.
- Provide manager-facing review summaries.
- Generate internal follow-up coaching based on real consultation transcript.

## What SkillⅡ Does Not Do

- Does not diagnose the customer from scratch.
- Does not regenerate or rewrite SkillⅠ reports.
- Does not replace SkillⅠ.
- Does not crawl jobs automatically.
- Does not promise Offer, salary, referral, interview, admission, or service result.
- Does not fabricate job information, customer experience, market data, product facts, pricing, or service scope.
- Does not act as a fixed script library.
- Does not grade consultants by whether they memorize a script.

## Evaluation Philosophy

Evaluate whether the consultant can complete the right consulting actions, use evidence, express boundaries, manage risk, and advance the customer honestly.

## v0.1 Non-Goals

- Automatic job crawling.
- Automatic industry or company database maintenance.
- Complex HTML report rendering.
- Customer-visible consultation reports.
- Full script-library construction.
- Long-term consultant performance dashboard.
- Cross-case big-data analytics.
